1. Why BAs Need SDLC Knowledge
To document requirements depending on SDLC model

To ensure developers build EXACTLY what business needs

To map requirements → deliverables

To support testing teams

2. SDLC Models
Model	BA Responsibilities	Why It’s Important
Waterfall	Write BRD, SRS upfront	Large documentation-heavy projects
Agile	Continuous requirement refinement	BA stays involved every sprint
Scrum	Support PO, clarify user stories	Ensures dev team understands acceptance criteria
Kanban	Update requirements continuously	Good for teams with constant changes
V-Model	Map requirements to test cases	Ensures 100% requirement validation
Spiral	Analyze risks at each iteration	Critical for uncertain projects
Iterative	Add improvements version-by-version	Ideal when business needs keep evolving

4. Quick BA Checklist

Adjust documentation depth accordingly

Validate all requirements before sprint starts

Maintain traceability from requirement → test

Communicate gaps early
